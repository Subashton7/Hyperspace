HYPERSPACE // SYSTEM SHAMAN  -  how to install (a field guide for mortals)
==========================================================================

Congratulations. Someone who loves you has handed you a program that makes
your laptop look like it's hacking the Matrix while it's actually checking
how hot it is. Let's begin the ritual.


STEP 1: Summon it
-----------------
Double-click HYPERSPACE-Setup-0.11.0.exe. (That one figures out what kind of PC
you have all by itself. If you were handed a smaller -x64 or -arm64 file
instead, use that: -x64 is nearly every PC, -arm64 is the Snapdragon crowd.)

It installs for your Windows account only, no admin password needed, and
quietly drops itself into the Start Menu and onto your desktop like a
houseguest who already knows where the snacks are.


STEP 2: Windows will panic. This is normal.
-------------------------------------------
You'll see "Windows protected your PC." Windows says this about every app
that didn't pay Microsoft for a fancy certificate. HYPERSPACE was made by a
DJ, not a corporation. Windows is being dramatic.

  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Windows will grumble. Ignore it. Windows grumbles about everything.


STEP 3: Power, only if you want it
----------------------------------
HYPERSPACE no longer begs for admin rights the moment it opens. It runs
politely as you. The day you click something that needs the keys to the
engine room (fan curves, power limits, the camera hard switch), it asks:
"Ask Windows now?" Say yes, Windows shows its "Do you want to allow…"
box, click Yes, done.

Hate being asked every launch? FANS page → SKIP ADMIN PROMPT. Only do
that on your own PC.

Click No and it still runs, just without the hardware wizardry. Like a
sports car with the engine politely asleep.

Bonus: Settings → LOOK → PURPLE ACCENTS, for when green alone isn't
dramatic enough.


WHAT WORKS ON WHICH LAPTOP (managing expectations)
--------------------------------------------------
Works on any Windows 10/11 PC, no questions asked:
  mic/camera kill switches, task manager, VJ visualizer, media deck,
  display switcher, shortcuts, widgets, asset router, CRATE, the phone link.

Acer Nitro / Predator only:
  fan curves, Acer thermal profiles, CPU temperature, the NitroSense key.
  The fan speeds were tuned on an Acer Nitro 5 (AN515-58). On a different
  Acer they'll still work, but treat the numbers like a horoscope: broadly
  right, emotionally accurate, not legally binding.

NVIDIA graphics only:
  GPU clocks, GPU temperature, VRAM tracking.

If your laptop can't do something, HYPERSPACE shows "N/A" instead of
pretending. It has integrity.


TIPS FROM THE SHAMAN
--------------------
- LOST? Press Ctrl+F (or the FIND button up top) and type what you want: "fans",
  "phone", "journal", "blackjack". It takes you straight there. No map required.

- COOLING (FANS page): five big buttons. AUTO, QUIET, BALANCED, COOL, MAX. Pick one.
  That's the whole job. FINE-TUNE is there if you enjoy drawing fan curves for fun.

- NOTES: an encrypted notebook + journal, laid out like OneNote (minus Microsoft).
  Everyday notes just open. The JOURNAL hides behind one master password, plus a
  recovery key to WRITE ON PAPER. Lose both and not even the shaman can read your
  diary. (Which is kind of the point.) Can import your OneNote notebooks in one click.

- TO-DO: a to-do list, plus "De-Big-Tech My Life": a quest line with XP, ranks and
  badges for escaping the big tech companies. Yes, quitting Google gives you XP now.

- GAMES: Blackjack, Poker (Hold'em, Five-Card Draw, video poker) and Spades with a bot
  partner. Play chips only. The bots don't gloat. Much.

- The startup animation is magnificent. If you hate joy, click or press any
  key to skip it, or go Burner -> HyperCore -> STARTUP ANIMATION and make it
  silent or turn it off.

- LITE MODE (the LITE button up top): when you're gaming, rendering, or
  running something heavy, HYPERSPACE turns off its own glitter (background,
  spinning things, glow) so your laptop can concentrate. On AUTO it does this
  by itself when the laptop gets busy or hot, and turns the sparkle back on
  once things calm down. Tweak the triggers in SETTINGS -> GENERAL.

- PHONE CONTROL: click LINK in the title bar and scan the QR code with your
  phone. A setup guide walks you through it one step at a time, like a very
  patient flight attendant. Everything between your phone and laptop is
  encrypted, and it only works on Wi-Fi networks you've told it to trust,
  so the coffee shop can't join the party.
  If Windows asks about the firewall, pick "Private networks" only.

- SAVE TO FILES (phone, MUSIC tab): send a whole playlist to your phone's
  Files app as a .zip, or hold one track to grab just that song.

- The asset router is OFF until you switch it on (ROUTER tab). It will not
  start rearranging your files behind your back. We're not like that.

- SPOUT OUT (VJ tab): flip it on and the visualizer appears as a source
  called "HYPERSPACE" in Resolume (Sources -> Spout), OBS (Spout2 Capture),
  TouchDesigner (Syphon Spout In) and friends. Pick the same graphics card the
  other app uses (usually NVIDIA), or they'll stare at each other in silence.

- LIVE WALLPAPER (VJ tab): the visualizer plays behind your desktop icons and
  dances to whatever's playing. It politely pauses when a game or video goes
  fullscreen, because it has manners.

- CRATE (music page): if you have rekordbox 6 or 7 it reads your whole library
  (BPM, key, cues, playlists). No rekordbox? Click + ADD and point it at a
  music folder like a normal person.

- CRATE -> DJ DECKS: two decks with EQ kills, filter, crossfader, sync and
  LINE UP DROPS. SETTINGS -> CRATE -> FETCH FOR MY LIBRARY pulls artwork,
  release dates and tags from SoundCloud.

- TABS: Ctrl+click a page in the sidebar to open it in a new tab. Drag a tab
  out of the window (or click its window icon) and it becomes its own window,
  free at last.

- SETTINGS -> DISPLAY: every background effect lives here (rain, code
  waterfall, tunnel, spirals...). Go wild. Or go LITE. Your call.

- Acer Nitro only: BURNER -> HyperCore -> NITROSENSE KEY makes the NitroSense
  button open HYPERSPACE instead. NitroSense will be sad. That's okay.


BREAKING UP WITH HYPERSPACE
---------------------------
Windows Settings -> Apps -> Installed apps -> HYPERSPACE -> Uninstall.

It won't text you. It won't make it weird. It'll just quietly go back into
the void it came from.
